star-trails support

Help for the Star Trails app for iPhone and iPad. It stacks photos or a video into one star trail image, all on your device.

Contact

Email hi@javier.xyz with questions, problems or ideas. It helps to include your device model, its iOS or iPadOS version, and what you were trying to import or export.

Requirements

An iPhone or iPad running iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, or later.

What it works with

  • Input: photos or a video, opened from Photos or Files. Videos are sampled to at most 600 frames.
  • Controls: Range picks which frames are stacked. Fade sets how the trails fade out.
  • Export: a JPEG, a video or a Live Photo.
JPEG and Live Photo exports can keep the metadata of the first selected frame, including its GPS location. See the privacy policy for how to share without it.

Saving to Photos doesn't work

The app needs permission to add photos before it can save an export. If you chose Don't Allow, you can turn it back on:
  • Open the Settings app.
  • Go to Apps › Star Trails › Photos.
  • Choose Add Photos Only.
This only lets the app add new items. It can't see or change your library. Opening photos doesn't need this permission, because you pick them yourself.

Running out of storage

While you work, the app keeps temporary copies of your imports, the frames it samples from videos, and your exports. Many full-size photos or a long video can take a lot of space.
  • Check your free space in Settings › General › iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage), and free up some if it's low.
  • Import fewer photos, or a shorter video.
  • With iCloud Photos set to Optimize Storage, full-size originals are downloaded when you import them, so they need room too.

Photos or videos won't import

  • If they are in iCloud, connect to the internet and wait for them to download. In Files, items with a cloud icon need to be downloaded first.
  • Make sure there is enough free space (see above).
  • Try a few items at a time to find out if one file is the problem.
  • If a file uses an unusual format, export it from its original app as a JPEG photo or a standard video and try again.
  • Update to the latest version of iOS or iPadOS and of the app.
If it still doesn't work, email me and say what kind of file it is and where it came from.

Privacy

The app has no account, analytics, tracking, ads or uploads. Read the full privacy policy.
