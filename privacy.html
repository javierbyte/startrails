star-trails privacy

This policy covers the Star Trails app for iPhone and iPad. The website has its own section below. Last updated November 8, 2026.

The short version

The app does not collect any data. There is no account or login, and no analytics, tracking, ads or uploads. Your photos and videos are processed on your device. They are never sent to me or anyone else.

Your photos and videos

You choose what to open with the system Photos and Files pickers. The app only sees the items you pick, not the rest of your library or your files.
When you save an export to Photos, iOS asks for permission to add photos. This is add-only permission. It lets the app save new items, but not see or change anything already in your library.

What stays on your device

While you work, the app keeps copies of your imported items, the frames it samples from videos, and your exports in its private storage on your device. These temporary files are deleted afterwards. Deleting the app also removes anything left in its storage.
Exports you save to Photos or Files are yours. They stay where you put them until you delete them.

Location and other metadata in exports

JPEG and Live Photo exports can keep the metadata of the first selected frame. This can include the camera, the date and time, and the GPS location where it was taken. If you share an export, that information goes with it.
To share without the location, tap Options at the top of the share sheet in Photos and turn off Location. You can also remove the location from a saved photo in Photos. Swipe up on the photo, tap Adjust next to the location, and choose No Location.

Sharing with others

The app collects nothing, so there is nothing to share with anyone. It has no analytics or advertising services and does not sell or share data. The App Store label for the app is Data Not Collected.
Apple handles downloads and updates under Apple's privacy policy. If you turned on Share With App Developers in your device's Analytics settings, Apple may send me crash reports and anonymous usage totals. That data comes from Apple, not from the app, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Your choices

  • To remove the app's permission to add photos, go to Settings › Apps › Star Trails › Photos and choose None.
  • To delete everything the app stores on your device, delete the app.
  • I hold no data about you from the app, so there is nothing for me to delete or hand over.

If you email me

If you write to hi@javier.xyz, I receive your email address and anything you include in the message, like screenshots. I use it only to reply to you. I don't share it, and I'll delete it if you ask.

This website

This section is about the website, not the app. The pages under javier.xyz/startrails, including this one and the web version of Star Trails, use Google Analytics to count visits. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives information such as the pages you view, your browser and device, and your approximate location based on your IP address. Google handles this data under its own policies. You can block it with your browser's cookie or content blocking settings, or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Vercel. Like any web host, they process the network details needed to deliver each page, such as your IP address. The web tool processes your photos and videos in your browser and does not upload them.
